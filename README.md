# Manuel / tofusito

I build small, practical systems for my own life: self-hosted tools, AI agents, personal finance software, and voice-first apps. Most of my public repos are either working tools I use or research notes from things I am actively exploring.

My portfolio lives at [me.tofusito.org](https://me.tofusito.org).

## Building

- [Kakei](https://github.com/tofusito/kakei) - self-hosted personal finance tracker with a minimal, privacy-first interface.
- [Ming](https://github.com/tofusito/ming) - personal self-hosted voice translator for Mandarin Chinese and Spanish family conversations.

## Research

- [Multi Agents](https://github.com/tofusito/multi-agents) - research notes and working setups for cost-aware multi-agent development workflows.
- [Agent Forge](https://github.com/tofusito/agents) - practical custom AI agents for personal workflows and experiments.

## Homelab

- [Homelab](https://github.com/tofusito/homelab) - reference Docker setup for media, automation, networking, and self-hosted services.

## Apps and Experiments

- [Hera](https://github.com/tofusito/hera), [Pika](https://github.com/tofusito/pika), and [Xun](https://github.com/tofusito/xun) are older public experiments. I keep them visible as snapshots of ideas, prototypes, and product directions I explored.
